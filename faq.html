Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services

What types of shutters do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of shutter solutions including galvanized shutters for industrial use, vertical blinds for large windows, venetian blinds for classic style, and roller blinds for modern interiors. Each type comes in various colors, materials, and sizes to meet your specific requirements.

Do you serve government offices and NGOs?

Yes, we have extensive experience working with government offices, NGOs, hotels, and hospitals across Ethiopia. We understand the specific requirements of institutional clients and provide customized solutions that meet regulatory standards while maintaining quality and durability.

How long does installation take?

Installation time varies depending on the project size and complexity. For standard residential installations, we typically complete the work within 1-2 days. Larger commercial projects may take 3-7 days. We always provide a detailed timeline during the consultation phase.

Do you offer warranties on your products?

Yes, all our products come with comprehensive warranties. Our galvanized shutters come with a 5-year warranty, while our blinds and other products have varying warranty periods based on the specific product and material. We stand behind the quality of our work and products.

Can I get a free estimate?

Absolutely! We offer free, no-obligation estimates for all our services. Simply contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or our contact form, and we'll schedule a consultation to understand your needs and provide a detailed quote.

Do you provide custom sizes and colors?

Yes, we specialize in custom solutions. We can manufacture shutters and blinds in virtually any size to fit your windows perfectly. We also offer a wide range of colors and finishes to match your interior design preferences.

How can I contact you for a project?

You can reach us through multiple channels: call us at 09 13 00 81 64 or 09 12 37 71 11, send an email to gezahegntad6266@gmail.com, or use our contact form on this website. We respond to all inquiries within 2 hours during business hours.

Do you service areas outside Addis Ababa?

Yes, we primarily serve clients in Addis Ababa and surrounding areas. For projects outside the capital, we can discuss travel arrangements and logistics during the consultation phase to ensure we can meet your needs.
