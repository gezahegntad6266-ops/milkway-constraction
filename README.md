# Gezahegn Plastic & Metal Shutter Works

A professional, mobile-responsive website for Gezahegn Shutter Works, a company specializing in galvanized shutters, vertical blinds, venetian blinds, and roller blinds serving hotels, hospitals, government offices, and NGOs in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

## 📋 Features

- **Fully Responsive Design** - Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Interactive Navigation** - Hamburger menu for mobile devices with smooth scrolling
- **Service Showcase** - Clear display of all shutter services with icons and badges
- **Google Maps Integration** - Shows business location at Tracon Tower, Piassa, Addis Ababa
- **Contact Form** - Working contact form with client-side validation
- **Client Portfolio** - Highlights target clientele (Hotels, Hospitals, Government, NGOs)
- **Professional Design** - Clean, trust-inspiring color scheme with dark blue and gold
