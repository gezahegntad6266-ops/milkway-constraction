About Gezahegn Shutter Works

Established in 2010, Gezahegn Plastic and Metal Shutter Works has grown to become a trusted name in the shutter industry across Ethiopia.

We specialize in providing high-quality galvanized shutters, vertical blinds, venetian blinds, and roller blinds for a wide range of clients.
