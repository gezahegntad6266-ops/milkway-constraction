About Gezahegn Shutter Works

Established in 2010, Gezahegn Plastic and Metal Shutter Works has grown to become a trusted name in the shutter industry across Ethiopia. We specialize in providing high-quality galvanized shutters, vertical blinds, venetian blinds, and roller blinds for a wide range of clients.

Our commitment to precision, durability, and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for hotels, hospitals, government offices, and NGOs. We combine modern technology with skilled craftsmanship to deliver solutions that stand the test of time.
